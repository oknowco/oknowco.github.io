Scar credits

Scar is an interactive reconstruction of the building of Scar House Dam in Nidderdale. The landscape, buildings and work routes include interpretations based on maps, photographs and surveys.

Landscape and historical references

  • Terrain derived from the Environment Agency LiDAR DTM. © Environment Agency copyright and/or database right 2022. Open Government Licence v3.0.
  • Moorland cover guided by Natural England’s Priority Habitats Inventory. © Natural England. Contains Ordnance Survey data © Crown copyright and database right 2024.
  • Distant ground cover uses modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2025, from Sentinel-2C scene S2C_30UWF_20250513_0_L2A.
  • Railway and shoreline informed by © OpenStreetMap contributors under the Open Database License.
  • Historical references include the Scar Navvy Camp survey, National Railway Museum construction photographs, and British Pathé footage.

Models, textures and film

  • The elephant adapts the African Elephant Bull by AnimalAnimator.com under CC BY 4.0. The model was resized, its ears made smaller, and its textures reduced for the browser. The game uses its walk clip.
  • Sheep Skull by Slipdogg, captured with Polycam, under CC BY 4.0. The scan was scaled and positioned for the tunnel figure.
  • Grazing sheep use tevy Sheep v1. The child model adapts the base mesh, morphs and rig from MakeHuman for Blender with clothing and hair made for Scar. Both are based on CC0 assets.
  • Photographic material scans, rocks and the overcast sky use Poly Haven assets under CC0.
  • The cinema plays a silent excerpt from a public-domain print of The Great Train Robbery (1903).

Sound

  • 20170618_dripping.cave.wav by dobroide (CC BY 4.0) and cave dripping water by mentos987 (CC0) were trimmed, filtered, level-adjusted and mixed to mono for Armathwaite Tunnel.
  • Field recordings include wind by Sandy Ogilvie, a hoist by kyles, and sheep bleating by earthcalling.
  • Footsteps use grass and gravel recordings by Fission9, wooden floor footsteps by nicotep, and stone footsteps by SecureSubset.
